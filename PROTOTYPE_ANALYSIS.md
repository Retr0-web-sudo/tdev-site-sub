# TDEV Wix Prototype Analysis

## Site Structure
The prototype is a minimalist fashion/lifestyle brand website for "Tenue de Ville" (TDEV).

### Pages
1. **TDEV (Home)** - Hero with "TENUE DE VILLE" text overlay on textured background
2. **FEMME** - Hero with "SPRING 2017" and jellyfish imagery (high-key, ethereal aesthetic)
3. **HOMME** - Hero with "SPRING 2017" and stacked stones on beach (low-key, natural aesthetic)
4. **GLOBAL** - Gallery of 4 images (family, architecture, sunset, mountain landscape)
5. **ABOUT** - Dark background with centered text about the brand's mission
6. **CONTACT** - Contact form with address information and location details

## Design Elements

### Navigation
- Vertical sidebar navigation on the left (TDEV, FEMME, HOMME, GLOBAL, ABOUT, CONTACT)
- Minimalist, clean typography
- White text on dark backgrounds

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep blacks and dark grays
- **Accents**: White/light gray text
- **Secondary**: Muted earth tones (beige/tan for form elements)

### Typography
- Large, bold serif or sans-serif display text ("SPRING 2017", "TENUE DE VILLE")
- Elegant, minimal body text
- Generous letter-spacing for luxury feel

### Layout
- Full-width hero sections with background images
- Centered content with asymmetric layouts
- Minimalist footer with email subscription and social links
- Clean form elements with subtle styling

### Visual Style
- High-quality photography (nature, lifestyle, fashion)
- Minimalist, sophisticated aesthetic
- Emphasis on whitespace and breathing room
- Subtle animations and transitions

## Key Features to Implement
1. Multi-page navigation system
2. Hero sections with background images
3. Email subscription form
4. Contact form
5. Social media links (Facebook, Pinterest)
6. Responsive design
7. Smooth page transitions

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Tenue de Ville (Outfit of the City)
- **Tagline**: Focus on social and cultural awareness
- **Mission**: Foster creativity, inclusion, and humanitarianism
- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist, sophisticated, nature-inspired
